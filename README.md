DESIPAISAKHATA – PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. INTRODUCTION
Welcome to DesiPaisaKhata ("we", "our", or "us"). This Mobile App is owned and operated by RAJESH DATTA acting as an Individual Independent Developer. We are deeply committed to protecting your personal data privacy rights. This Privacy Policy outlines exactly how we handle user data parameters for our mobile expense tracking software application.

## 2. DATA WE COLLECT AND WHY
We believe in strict data minimization. The only user infrastructure properties we handle are:
- **Email Address:** Collected solely during your initial registration setup to uniquely identify your secure premium account profile mapping and handle validation access and deliver automated email reports.
- **Expense Entries:** Any expense entry with the respective amount or cost and notes you type into the application are compiled and stored safely.

## 3. DATA STORAGE, ISOLATION, AND CLOUD SYNCING
- **Trial Tier (100% Offline Storage):** For users utilizing our Ten-Day Free Trial, all transaction data, expense categories, amount records, and sub-category inputs are stored 100% locally inside your physical mobile device's private SQLite database. No trial transaction records are ever uploaded, tracked, or stored on our cloud servers.
- **Premium Tier (Secure Azure Cloud Syncing):** For verified Premium Tier accounts, data rows are transported securely over encrypted network client channels (HTTPS) and mirrored onto enterprise-grade cloud database servers powered by Microsoft Azure and Azure SQL. This allows you to seamlessly switch mobile hardware and download your expense history onto a new phone in seconds.
- **Network Alert Integrity:** DesiPaisaKhata does not require constant internet connectivity. Any category transaction with its cost expense can be saved safely offline. Whenever an active network operation is strictly required by the application (such as authentication or cloud syncing), you will instantly receive a clear alert on your screen.

## 4. DATA PRIVACY AND THIRD-PARTY POLICY
- **Zero SMS Scraping:** Unlike alternative tools, DesiPaisaKhata does NOT read, scan, or scrape your private mobile network SMS data lines or banking transaction texts.
- **Zero Data Selling:** Your data, registration emails, and tracking analytics are completely private. We never share, trade, rent, or sell your data to third-party advertising algorithms.
- **Payment Data Safety:** All  transaction and subscription processing are driven directly via the secure Razorpay payment gateway API and Google Play Billing infrastructure. We do not store credit card integers or local bank branch passwords.

## 5. AUTOMATED REPORTING
To help you stay completely on top of your household expenses, our server system compiles automated weekly (Monday summaries) and monthly reports. These summary files are delivered automatically straight to your registered email inbox via our secure cloud email delivery pipeline.

## 6. USER CONTROL AND AUTOMATED DELETION RIGHTS
You possess full structural rights over your profile metadata. DesiPaisaKhata provides a fully automated data elimination system to ensure you maintain complete ownership. You can trigger a full database wipe for your user email specific records  directly by clicking the "Delete Account" button inside the app settings panel. This action instantly purges your local device history and broadcasts a secure terminal command to completely erase your user profile records from our Azure cloud sync tables in real time.
